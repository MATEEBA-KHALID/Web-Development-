About the Library

The university library supports students and faculty by providing a learning space for reading, research and academic study. Please check the campus library for current timings and services.

Library Facilities

  • Books and reference material
  • Individual study spaces
  • Research and academic resources
  • Reading area for students

How to use the library

  1. Bring your university identification card.
  2. Search the catalogue or ask library staff for help.
  3. Keep the environment quiet and return borrowed items on time.

Library Rules

Keep your phone silent, handle books carefully, and respect other readers. Food and drink policies should be confirmed with library staff.
